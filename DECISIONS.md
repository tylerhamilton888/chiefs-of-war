# Design decisions

| Subject | Confirmed | Still open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | A short, authored prologue in the player's named clan. Ordinary life, hunt and feast precede a downfall rooted in complacency and poor leadership. The survivor begins independent travel with a wagon and a beast. | Specific missed warnings, attacker, scene order, length and the terms of departure. Preserve existing rescue and duel outcomes where applicable. |
| Character | The player creates a male Ogre with a name, clan, background and visual identity. One stable playable body supports equipment across poses. Ogres are functionally immortal and retain physical capability with age. | Number of face presets and backgrounds, customization coverage and exact modular slots. Eight to twelve faces is a working art target. |
| Combat | WASD locomotion, cursor aim, relaxed travel facing, combat strafing and retreat. Basic movement, attacks and defenses stay available. A clean cleaver hit shears apart an exposed unarmored goblin. Eating or gathering its remains is deliberate. | Timings, input transitions, precise threat thresholds, destruction fixtures and enemy composition. |
| Progression | XP, levels and loot accompany multiple kinds of dietary experience. Foods and preparations earn resources to unlock permanent abilities. A limited selection of active and passive talents can be equipped. Meal recovery and temporary benefits coexist with permanent unlocks. | Dietary categories, award rates, costs, loadout capacity, hunger cadence, preservation and exceptional creature-derived talents. |
| Food | Distinct meat, organs and unusual ingredients have uses in meals, travel, trade, hospitality and suitable beast diets. Recipes are learnable. Cooking uses reusable hands-on actions and allows eventual delegation. | Exact ingredients and dishes. Ghost marrow, vampire blood or troll preparations are examples for testing. |
| Household and beasts | A persistent portable home. Camp packing preserves layout and possessions. Bonded animals can travel, hunt or work with the player. Solo combat is viable. Routine incapacitation is recoverable for beasts and adventuring companions. | Active-companion limits, hauling friction, first beast's precise role, breeding and training depth. |
| World | Free movement on a living illustrated 2D map leads into elevated three-quarter local scenes. Employers offer negotiable work, extended campaigns and optional allegiance. Conduct affects particular people and factions. | Simulation frequency, faction roster, map abstraction, encounter density and later political systems. |
| Visuals | Mature classic-fantasy color, bear-like strongman anatomy, upper canines, coherent whole-body action, physically grounded modular scenery. Personal narrative imagery reflects the player's actual look and gear or uses an appropriate viewpoint. | Camera tuning, technical animation pipeline, frame counts, finalized controls for zoom and exact equipment layer order. |
| Production | One Ogre campaign. The tutorial is the integrated slice. A direct-access Developer Arena uses shared gameplay and disposable state. Existing project and save lineage continue. | Balance, hardware targets and human acceptance of individual animations and scenes. |

The Art & Animation Bible sets the visual gates. Proposed cultures, supernatural recipes, political careers and additional companions can enter the roadmap when the preceding playable work supports them.
