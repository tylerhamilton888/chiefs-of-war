# Iron maul swing drawings

The two source sheets were generated as full-body illustrations using the equipped Ogre's existing Nordic maul sheet as the visual reference. The side sheet supplies the coiled load, high descending pass, low finish and return. The other sheet supplies the descending pass and low finish from the front and rear. The other key drawings come from the existing registered maul artwork.

The campaign runtime atlas is `assets/chapter/player_maul_swing.png`. The eight drawings have transparent gutters. Each entry in the three directional swing JSON files records a rectangle and local foot anchor; a null entry uses the original whole-body key pose for that stage. The left-facing view mirrors the complete right-facing drawing. No moving weapon overlay is involved.
