# Hearth and Hunger

An illustrated, three-quarter-view Ogre RPG made in Godot 4.4.1. The campaign opens with *The Fire We Carry*. Help Bramm repair the family wagon at Emberhide Crossing, go on your first hunt, and carry the household through the burning of Emberhide to the Stonebelly Host. The same journey continues when you earn a wagon of your own.

Open `project.godot` and run the main scene. Choose **Begin Journey** to start or **Continue** to resume. The title, journal, roads and personal wagon all belong to the same campaign. The campaign uses the existing `fire_we_carry_v1.json` save. Retired Orc and Frontier saves are left on disk and can still be opened with their original versions.

WASD moves, left click swings the axe toward the cursor, right click guards or parries, and Space dodges. Press E to interact, Q to eat, J for routes and objectives, I for belongings, F5 to save, and Esc to pause.
