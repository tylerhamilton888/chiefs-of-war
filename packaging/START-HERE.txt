CHIEFS OF WAR

PLAY THE GAME
1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click ChiefsOfWar.exe (Windows may show it as ChiefsOfWar).

That is the game. You do not need Godot, an installer, or a source project.
Keep the extracted folder somewhere convenient. You can create a desktop
shortcut to ChiefsOfWar.exe if you like.

YOUR CHARACTER
A new game opens character creation. An existing game continues your save.
Press Esc, then Appearance, to change your existing Orc's look.
Downloading a new build does not erase your campaign.

CONTROLS
RMB: hold to block toward the cursor; time the press just before impact to parry.
WASD: move       Left mouse: swing axe       Space: dodge
E: interact     I: inventory                J: journal
Esc: pause      F5: save                    C: switch owned outfits
F: earned finisher when alongside a vulnerable raider
Shift+F: optional goblin cleave

OPTIONAL PRACTICE
Extras contains Combat Practice.bat. It opens a separate, unsaved practice
yard. It never loads or changes your campaign. Use ChiefsOfWar.exe for the
normal game.

Your campaign is stored separately from this download in:
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Chiefs of War\ancestral_hearth_v1.json

Build information and engine notices are in Support.

MAIN MENU & CHARACTERS
Choose New character in one of three empty slots. Play loads that slot.
Continue resumes the last selected character. Your original save is slot one.
Esc > Main menu / characters saves before returning to the slot menu.
Practice is separate and never writes campaign saves.
