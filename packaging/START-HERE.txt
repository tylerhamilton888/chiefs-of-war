HEARTH AND HUNGER — THE FIRE WE CARRY

1. Use Windows "Extract All" on the downloaded ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click HearthAndHunger.exe.
3. Choose "The Fire We Carry · Chapter One".

Start as a young Ogre at your family's traveling hearth. Work, hunt and feast;
face the fall of Emberhide; earn a place in the Stonebelly Host and eventually
a wagon and hearth of your own.

WASD: move    E: interact    LMB: attack toward cursor
RMB: hold to block toward cursor; press just before impact to parry
Space: dodge    Q: eat meal    J: journal/routes    I: belongings
F5: save    Esc: pause
Dialogue: Space/Enter reveals and advances; Esc skips to final choices.
Household: pack at your wagon, travel, then B settles in a safe region.

Chapter One autosaves independently. Earlier campaigns and the first Ogre
visit remain available from the title; they are never overwritten by this chapter.
The optional combat practice launcher is in Extras. It uses the original campaign.
Build/source information and engine notices are in Support.

This is the first playable Chapter One release. Expanded equipment, marriage,
children and a full dynasty simulation are later milestones.
