HEARTH AND HUNGER

Extract the entire ZIP and run HearthAndHunger.exe. Select Begin Journey or
Continue. Your journey opens at Emberhide Crossing with Bramm and the wagon.

WASD moves. Aim with the mouse and left-click to swing the axe. Right-click
to guard or parry. Space dodges. E interacts and Q eats a meal.
J opens your objectives and routes. I opens belongings. F5 saves.
Esc opens the pause menu, where you can save and return to the title.

Your existing Fire We Carry save resumes automatically through Continue.
Build information and engine notices are in Support.
