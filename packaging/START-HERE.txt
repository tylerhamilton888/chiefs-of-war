HEARTH AND HUNGER — SUNDERED FRONTIER PLAYTEST

1. Use Windows "Extract All" on the downloaded ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click HearthAndHunger.exe.
3. Choose "Hunt · Fight · Feast — Sundered Frontier" on the title screen.

You are an Ogre with a wandering hearth. Fight, hunt, carry heavy loads, care
for Flint and take contracts. This is an early playtest; balance and animation
will benefit from your feedback.

WASD: move     E: interact     LMB: light attack toward cursor
R or Shift + LMB: heavy attack     RMB: hold to block / time a parry
Space: dodge     Q: eat a meal     X: set a carried load down
F: throw a staggered goblin or clinch a vulnerable Ogre champion
G: bite a vulnerable goblin     Shift + G: swallow a vulnerable goblin
J: journal/routes     I: belongings     F5: save     Esc: pause

Earn Ferocity by landing attacks or parrying. Feed and throw cost Ferocity;
stagger or critically wound the goblin first. Lift a whole trunk with E and
use the attack controls to swing it, or take it to your wagon. Open the pause
menu to select full or restrained blood effects.

"The Fire We Carry · Chapter One" remains on the title screen. It and the
frontier keep separate saves. Earlier campaigns remain available too. Optional
original-campaign combat practice is in Extras. Build/source information and
engine notices are in Support.
