HEARTH AND HUNGER — FIRST OGRE HEARTH

PLAY
1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click HearthAndHunger.exe.
No Godot installation is needed.

Choose Enter the wandering hearth for the new Ogre prototype.
WASD moves. E interacts. F5 saves. Esc opens the menu.
Collect fallen timber, take meat from your provisions, and cook at the hearth.
Your Ogre progress resumes on your next visit.

This first playable pivot contains movement, a campsite, collecting and cooking.
Ogre combat, equipping armor/weapons, wagon travel and family systems are pending.

EARLIER CHARACTERS
Earlier campaigns keeps your existing Chiefs of War slots, creator and combat.
Its controls: WASD move, LMB axe, RMB block/parry, Space dodge, E interact,
I belongings, J journal, F earned finisher, F5 save, Esc pause.
Extras/Combat Practice.bat opens the earlier unsaved combat yard.

YOUR SAVES
