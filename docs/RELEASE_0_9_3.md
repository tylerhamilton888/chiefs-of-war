# Iron and Embers

Select **Practice Yard** on the title screen. The unarmored goblin approaches from the left. Aim the hunting axe with the mouse and land a clean blow. Step up to the fallen cut and press **F** to eat it, or **E** to carry it. The cookpot stands just north of where you start. Press **E** at the pot to throw in the cut and make two portions. Ladle a portion to take it with you. A packed meal restores up to 65 health and grants the usual well-fed bonus. Eating the raw cut restores up to 25 health.

The same field cuts drop at Goblin Cairn in the campaign. Cuts left on the ground, cuts carried home and servings left in a particular pot survive saving and loading. The original hearth recipe and the family offering remain available. Restrained brutality presents the drop as a wrapped ration.

The iron helm and riveted scale mantle fit separately. Wear the mantle bareheaded, or keep the helm with the original fur. Both armor pieces are visual in this playtest. Your choice follows you through travel, fighting and the planted dodge. Older saves retain their appearance and weapon.

WASD moves, the mouse aims, and **Space** takes a short shoulder-first step out of danger. The hunting axe and iron maul each have a drawn crouch, drive and planted catch. Foreshortened steps cover movement toward or away from the camera. A successful blow holds the contact drawing briefly. The maul has a low impact sound and kicks sparks from an armored raider.

Open **Belongings** with **I** in the campaign to turn the Ogre and inspect your equipment. In the yard, **Tab** swaps weapons, **H** changes the helm, **B** changes the mantle, **R** resets the encounter and **Esc** returns to the title. Yard practice preserves campaign progress.
