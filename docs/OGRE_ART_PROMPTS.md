# Ogre pivot production prompts

Generated through the built-in imagegen skill, not the API/CLI. All selected assets are integrated into the Godot project. The two user-supplied boards were design inspiration; existing game architecture and menu paintings were the style references.

## Character

Production 4-column by 4-row full-body Ogre sprite atlas on genuine transparency. Bright chunky 2D inked classic strategy-game illustration, crisp dark outlines, broad warm colors and restrained texture. Upright enormous heavyweight strongman; barrel chest, substantial torso, huge hands, thick neck, powerful proportionate legs; tawny skin, broad nose and brow, lower tusks, tied-back reddish hair and braided beard. Cream fur mantle, bare torso, leather wrist wraps, broad belt with round bronze buckle, red cloth over dark knee-length trousers and leather boots. Empty hands. Same identity/outfit through south, west/east and rear directions; planted idle, left contact, passing and right contact poses. Complete bodies, separated cells, constant height/foot baseline, no detached tools, text or scenery.

Targeted revision requested alternating lead feet while preserving identity and layout. The result still repeated some foot contacts; runtime uses complete-pose mirroring for front/rear opposite contacts and west direction, with measured alpha bounds. These limitations are recorded rather than calling the gait final.

## Camp atlas

Four separate illustrated assets in a 2x2 atlas: huge four-wheel red/cream covered timber hearth wagon with rope lashings, broad wheels and removable steps; packable canvas shelter; iron cooking cauldron/tripod, contained fire and meat preparation stool; timber provisions chest, sacks, rolled canvas and firewood. Match the existing architecture's square-facing elevated camera, bold outlines, rich red cloth and golden timber. Clear silhouettes and broad shading, no masonry homes, people, animals, tiny repeated symbols or labels. Original single ember emblem.

Extraction revision requested fully transparent empty space, including ropes/wheels/tripod gaps, and no backdrop/glow. The output has genuine alpha. Godot uses reviewed nonrectangular atlas UV boundaries to prevent adjacent object fragments from appearing.

## Title background

Match the existing menu's bright chunky inked illustrated style, composition and warm light. Quiet open clearing on the left 60% for title controls; right side dominated by the new covered wagon, canvas shelter, portable cooking fire and supplies, with two smaller wagons behind. Bright mountain valley and pines, late-afternoon sky. No permanent houses, castles, CGI, sepia sketch treatment, text, logo, UI, people or animals.
