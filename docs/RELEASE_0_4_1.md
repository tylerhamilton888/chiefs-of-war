Expanded character creation and an appearance editor for existing characters: three faces, shaved/loose/swept-braid/twin-braid hair, independent beards and mustaches, eight hair colors, twelve skin tones and twelve cloth colors. Preview standing, walking and axe swings from four directions in either outfit. Iron remains earned in gameplay.

Open **Esc → Appearance** to edit an existing character. Cancel restores your prior appearance. Old saves keep their original head until you choose to edit it. Your campaign progress and location are preserved.

Includes recovered Axe and Iron combat improvements, optional earned finishers, gathering feedback and an isolated Combat-Practice launcher.

Validated with Godot 4.4.1 on Linux: customization persistence/legacy saves, opener, edge cases, movement, combat feel, execution mechanics, practice isolation, rendered creator/menu layouts, Windows export and packaged resource smoke test. Native Windows execution and human playfeel are not verified. Cosmetic artwork is a generated candidate; facial expression and hair movement are not independently animated.
