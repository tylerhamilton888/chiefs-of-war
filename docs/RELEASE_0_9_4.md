# A Bowl for Bramm

Choose **Begin Journey** to visit Emberhide Crossing on a fresh save, or **Continue** an early chapter. Bramm is tapping a peg into the near wheel. Across the clearing, Svala tends the cookpot. Listen from the wagon and from the fire to hear the difference.

Fetch the three bundles and repair the axle. Bramm sets the mallet aside. Bring meat and firewood to Svala’s pot, then speak to Bramm with a spare cooked meal and choose **Take a bowl, Father.** The first portion for Sava stays in your pack until offered. The gift spends one meal, raises Bramm’s friendship and is remembered on reload. Later saves continue in their current chapter.

The iron helm now has larger plates and a continuous brass brow that reads at gameplay zoom. Its size, face opening and compatibility with the armor and weapon selections remain consistent.
