# Hearth and Hunger 0.8.6 — Faces of the Clan

Ogre faces in the loading illustrations, dialogue portraits, story illustrations, player animations and clan walking sprites have been revised toward a Nordic-inspired look, with less exaggerated jaws and tusks. Their huge size, strength, individual character and existing clothing remain.

The bite-and-swallow animation retains its original artwork, and combat violence, blood, finishers, saves and gameplay remain intact.
