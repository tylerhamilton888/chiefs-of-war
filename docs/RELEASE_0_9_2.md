# Ogre in Motion

Choose **Practice Yard** on the title screen to get an axe in your hands immediately. Keep the cursor on a foe and use WASD to circle or back away. Left-click to attack. Tab equips the iron maul, R resets both opponents, and Esc returns to the title. The yard uses the campaign's controls and combat with disposable progress.

Both weapons have a drawn backswing, strike and recovery. The hunting axe reaches contact about 0.28 seconds into its 0.60-second swing. The iron maul reaches contact about 0.45 seconds into its 0.88-second swing. In full brutality, a killing axe hit on the unarmored goblin plays its illustrated cleave and fall. A maul kills the same goblin with an intact fall. The armored raider can take a hit and strike back.

Choose **Begin Journey** or **Continue** to try the travel gait at Emberhide Crossing. WASD walks and holding Shift away from combat runs. In a fight, the Ogre keeps his weapon drawn and faces the mouse as he advances, backs up or sidesteps. Inventory, appearance and chapter saves carry over from the previous build.
