# The Fire We Carry — opening outline

**Starting place:** Emberhide Crossing, the riverside camp of the player's clan. Emberhide remains the place name when the player chooses a clan name. Use the chosen identity in personal introductions. Keep authored NPC identities consistent. Keep the player's wagon in view early enough that taking a wagon out into the world later has personal meaning.

| Beat | Who and where | What the player does and why | Evidence in play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Life at the Hearth** | Bramm, the player's father and a hunter, at the family wagon. Svala keeps the nearby cooking fire. | Create the Ogre, arrive beside Bramm and learn which wheel needs work. Gather timber from the nearby stack and fit it to the axle. Bramm needs the wagon ready for the hunt. | An immediate objective names the task and the place. The player can find both by walking. The wheel and Bramm's reply show the result. Timber is spent once. Save, quit and return to the repaired wagon. |
| **The First Hunt** | Bramm and Dorrik on the trail from the Northern Pines to Wolfscar Hollow. Introduce the beast through a job it helps with. | Read signs, choose a route, meet ordinary opposition and a threat worth evading, then bring back supper. Dorrik's wager gives the player a reason to show off. A tame beast assists with tracking or hauling, while the quarry supplies food. | The player can distinguish a clean killing blow from a resisted hit, choose whether to gather the remains, and see what the beast contributed. |
| **The First Feast** | Svala at the cooking rig, followed by family and Gorund around the fire. | Deliver a known ingredient, prepare a dish, choose who receives a portion, then eat. The existing small meal after the axle repair can introduce the pot. Save the fuller hunt-to-feast payoff for the return. | Ingredients leave the pack and servings appear. A chosen dish shows its recovery and dietary experience. A permanent talent remains learned when the player changes their equipped talents. |
| **The Fallen Clan** | The crossing, familiar wagon paths and rescue positions. Gorund holds authority, Bramm and Dorrik have people and work to protect. | Dramatize a preventable mistake before the attack. One candidate is a warning about an unwatched approach dismissed before supper. Give the player a chance to intervene, defend, rescue or escape. Preserve valid existing rescue and duel outcomes. | The player can connect a decision made during ordinary life to what has gone wrong. People saved remain present. A player who wins the confrontation receives a coherent outcome. |
| **The Wandering Hearth** | The surviving household at a safe stopping place. Thyra, Runa and Eydis can offer hospitality, beast-handling knowledge and work through the Stonebelly Host. | Set out independently with a modest wagon and beast. Choose where to stop and whom to help. Turn host errands into opportunities along that road as the chapter is revised. | The first independent journey carries the chosen Ogre, saved possessions and beast. Packing and unpacking restores the household. The next outing is the player's choice. |

Gorund's exact mistake, the attacking force and the transition to independence remain narrative proposals. Retain the current save flags and consequences as details change.
