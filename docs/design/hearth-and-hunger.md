# Hearth and Hunger — governing pivot

The user's October 30 directive in ../source/HEARTH_AND_HUNGER_DIRECTIVE.txt supersedes the older Orc-centered vision for new development. Preserve the older implementation and its save files. The same Git repository remains in use; do not create a replacement project or rename the remote as a side effect.

## Visual acceptance

Retain OUR established colorful, chunky, inked, square-facing elevated 2D game style. The two attached Ogre / caravan boards are ideas for anatomy, clothing, occupations, family and mobile architecture, not a request to adopt their detailed sketch presentation. Reference files supplied in this turn: image(20261031-001902).png and image(20261031-001911).png. The source directive's art section is subordinate to this explicit clarification.

The male Ogre is an upright, huge strongman with a thick torso and powerful proportionate legs, broad nose/brow, tusks, large hands and Nordic-inspired hair. Do not recolor or enlarge an Orc and call it a new species. Men vary; avoid universal obesity. Female Ogres are recognizably the same people, feminine, imposing and attractive, with varied builds and important roles. Male-only initial player scope remains. Nomadic homes use enormous wagons, canvas/hide shelters, portable hearths and practical supplies. Permanent stone towns are not the default. Future independent weapons/armor must stay coherent through complete illustrated actions.

## Implementation order

0. Archive the existing work, retain systems/history, rename project/documentation.
1. A distinct playable Ogre, convincing directional movement, an attractive wilderness.
2. Excellent Ogre combat: responsive light/heavy strikes, defense, parries, dodges and one finisher; test crowds, rival Ogre and monster separately.
3. Hunting, harvesting and food.
4. Physical wagon/camp, deploy/pack, storage and upgrades.
5. One useful animated beast companion.
6. First NPC clan and autonomous work.

Do not infer that a decorative wagon is mobile or that a pregnant sprite simulates population. Finish a few actions before expanding the animation library. The new prototype currently has a movement/camp interaction slice; the milestone 1 quality bar is subject to user review. All later social, beast, political and prologue plans remain design intent. The complete directive is preserved without rewriting its detailed requirements.
