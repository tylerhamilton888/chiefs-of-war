# Chapter One — The Fire We Carry

Canonical source: `docs/source/FIRE_WE_CARRY_DIRECTIVE.txt`, the user's October 31 attachment. This extends the Ogre pivot in the same Godot project and keeps our established bright, chunky, outlined 2D style. The concept boards guide anatomy, culture and identity; they do not replace the rendering style.

Ogres never weaken merely through aging. Youth grows rapidly, adult growth slows but continues; ancient men and women may be their people's largest, strongest members. Injury, training, equipment and individual variation remain meaningful. Cast heights make this visible: young adults 155–168 units, mature adults 164–177, Thyra 205, Gorund 220, Hrothgar 242. These are present art-scale decisions, not a completed aging simulation.

## Playable chapter

| Step | Activity | Persistent result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gather six timber, repair Bramm's axle; optional Dorrik wager | Wagon repaired, resources spent, family admiration |
| 2 | Take supplies, cook, eat, offer Sava a portion | Meals consumed, health/strength bonus, religious custom |
| 3 | Read three signs, travel into Wolfscar Hollow, fight and harvest the alpha | Real combat victory, meat/hides/trophy |
| 4 | Supply the feast; speak to family and Gorund; choose when to leave | Explicit readiness, no forced immediate attack |
| 5 | Return to smoke; defeat hired blades and reach the two rescue positions before time expires | Bramm and Dorrik each escape or fall; later cast changes |
| 6 | Duel Hrothgar with valid damage and telegraphed attacks | Mercy after defeat, acknowledged terms after victory |
| 7 | Meet Thyra and Runa at the larger traveling host | New work and relationship opportunities |
| 8 | Clear the pass, feed ore to a stone-eater, physically escort a wagon | Road opened, beast trust, paid work |
| 9 | Negotiate Eydis's contract, defeat the three goblins, settle payment | Bargain remembered, coin and reputation |
| 10 | Supply and buy a personal wagon | Independent hearth; optional work, courtship, hunting and travel continue |

All nine principals have individual appearance data, sprites, portraits and dialogue. Bramm and Dorrik can accompany the first hunt. Lightweight patrol/work routes keep camp characters moving; these are not a full daily-life simulation. Astrid's recovered family pendant, Runa's fitted harness and Eydis's forge commission are distinct follow-up activities. Courtship is an explicit adult choice, separate from friendship. Marriage, children and dynasties are deliberately not represented as implemented.

## Geography

Seven loaded gameplay regions joined by marked walkable road exits. The player physically approaches an exit and interacts. No overworld pawn. Emberhide is a small riverside family camp. Stonebelly contains several much larger wagons, tents, fires and beasts. Blackstone reuses permanent timber settlement architecture for the smaller peoples. Resource deposits and wilderness encounters support repeat visits; long-term ecology is future work.

## Cinematics

Godot runs camera pans, physical character staging, whole-body poses, written dialogue and original generated illustrated panels. No external videos. The fall of Gorund uses the broken torc, ancestral axe and covered body: brutal political consequence with no graphic anatomy. Optional stylized blood affects combat impacts. The six illustrations cover burning camp, Gorund's fall, challenge, mercy, host journey and personal hearth.

The scene cursor is saved. Skip jumps to the final choices and never silently chooses romance or a different outcome. Gameplay pauses while dialogue is open, including the rescue timer. Every narrative mutation belongs to the story/action layer rather than a cosmetic tween.
