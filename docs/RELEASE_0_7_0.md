# Hearth and Hunger 0.7.0 — The Fire We Carry

Chapter One is now playable from the Emberhide family camp through the loss of the clan, joining Stonebelly's host, and earning a personal wagon and hearth.

Download **HearthAndHunger-Windows-0.7.0.zip**, Extract All, run **HearthAndHunger.exe**, and choose **The Fire We Carry · Chapter One**. This chapter uses its own save. Earlier campaigns and the first Ogre visit remain available and unchanged.

**Controls:** WASD move; LMB axe toward the cursor; hold RMB to block toward the cursor (the first 0.18 seconds parry); Space dodge; E interact; Q eat; J journal/routes; I belongings; F5 save; Esc pause. In dialogue, Space reveals/advances and Esc goes to the final choices. Pack your household at its wagon, travel, then press B in a safe region to settle it.
