# Life at the Hearth

Begin Journey creates a male Ogre with a name, clan, starting background and hair color. Continue resumes an existing campaign. Bramm needs timber to set the family wagon right at Emberhide Crossing. Follow the work through the repair and back to the family hearth.

Walk through the encampment with WASD and hold Shift to move briskly. Open Belongings with I to try the hunting axe and iron maul. A disposable developer encounter exercises the campaign actors, equipment and combat resolution.

This build begins the opening playtest. Appearance options use one existing illustrated body, and equipment currently covers the two weapons. The wider art direction, full wardrobe, hunting and dietary progression remain in development.
