# Current design decisions

- The player is an Ogre. The established bright, chunky, outlined 2D art and elevated camera are the gameplay reference. Preserve complete body poses, powerful upright anatomy and upper fangs.
- The Fire We Carry opens a single campaign about an Ogre and his Wandering Hearth. Build expeditions, household improvements and new regions into that journey. A developer arena is a testing tool with isolated state.
- Use WASD movement with mouse aim. Ordinary attacks, movement and defenses remain available without stamina, hunger or a compulsory Ferocity meter. The existing chapter's guard costs are part of the upcoming combat revision.
- The combat target is a clean cleaver hit that shears apart an ordinary unarmored goblin. Eating pieces is deliberate. The player can instead gather goblin meat for the camp pot or leave it behind. This belongs to the upcoming combat milestone.
- Preserve the original chapter save path and schema. Old campaigns are recoverable through Git history and releases. Their save files remain separate.
