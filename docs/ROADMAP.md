# Development order

Make each milestone playable through the same title, character and campaign save. The arena provides a shorter route to investigate a particular action. Raw captures, short normal-speed clips and save checks accompany the milestones they exercise.

| Milestone | Work | Playable exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Life at the Hearth** | One created Ogre, a small authored cast, grounded camp, first interaction, visible equipment and clear immediate work. | Create an Ogre, walk through the camp, speak with Bramm, finish a household task, change supported equipment and return to the same identity and state after reloading. Review movement and equipment at gameplay zoom. |
| **2. The First Hunt** | A beast on a local trail, trackable prey, ordinary and resistant combatants, a useful destructible obstacle and recoverable spoils. | Complete the outing through normal controls. Demonstrate impact, defense, beast contribution and gathering. Reproduce the fight in the arena and reset it with campaign saves untouched. |
| **3. The First Feast** | A known recipe, hands-on preparation, recovery and dietary experience, a small selection of learnable and equippable talents. | Turn one hunt's ingredients into a visibly prepared dish and a lasting character choice. Show ingredients, rewards and talent state before cooking and after reloading. |
| **4. The Fallen Clan** | Missed warning or poor judgment, loss, response and a transition founded on existing kinship and consequences. | Play from household life through the downfall. Resolve rescue or confrontation choices, then begin independent life coherently. Review the story with a visibly customized Ogre. |
| **5. The Wandering Hearth** | Persistent wagon and beast, camp layout, a small living-map journey and an independent opportunity. | Pack, travel, encounter another actor, camp and resume the same household. A return visit retains the outcome of the outing. |

After these proofs, add culturally distinctive contracts and equipment, broader world simulation and deeper household careers according to what the player actually enjoys. Production counts, frame counts and mission duration follow playtests. [Visual acceptance gates](art/ART_AND_ANIMATION_BIBLE.md)
