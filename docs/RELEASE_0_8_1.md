# Hearth and Hunger 0.8.1 — Choose Your Ogre

The Sundered Frontier now starts with an Ogre creator. Give your Ogre a name and select copper, dark, gold or silver beard coloring with a live preview. The selection carries across movement, combat, hauling, and finishers. These are color variations of the same illustrated body and hairstyle, not independent face or hairstyle sculpts; some red accents share the palette.

Returning frontier players keep their current save and default appearance. Pause and choose Change your Ogre’s name and look to edit without resetting progress. Chapter One remains separate and keeps its existing protagonist.

This is an early playtest. Sundered Frontier includes combat, hunting, feeding finishers, a wieldable trunk, Flint, contracts, and a portable hearth. Balance, animation quality and the wider world remain work in progress.
