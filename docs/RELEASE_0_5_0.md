Hearth and Steel adds a main menu with Continue, three independent character slots, Controls and unsaved Practice. Your existing campaign remains in slot one at its original path. Empty slots open character creation; occupied slots load their own world and checkpoints. Esc → Main menu / characters saves before switching. Unreadable saves are preserved and cannot be replaced through New character.

Hold RMB toward an attacker to block. Press just before impact for a timed parry that prevents damage and staggers the attacker. Blocking consumes guard and allows a little chip damage; repeated heavy hits break guard. Attacks from behind bypass it. Release guard to recover. Space still dodges; late attack recovery can transition into guard. Both outfits have new whole-body block and dodge poses across four directions.
