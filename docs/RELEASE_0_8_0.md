# Hearth and Hunger 0.8.0 — Sundered Frontier

A work-in-progress playtest of the Ogre frontier. From the title choose Hunt · Fight · Feast. The released Chapter One remains accessible and has a separate save.

Fight goblins and an Ogre champion, hunt the ridgeback, haul cargo, care for Flint, build up a traveling hearth and take contracts. Carry a whole trunk to fight with it: left click swings, R or Shift+left click delivers a heavier blow. Stagger or critically wound a goblin and earn Ferocity through hits and counters; G bites off an arm and Shift+G swallows the goblin whole. F throws a staggered goblin. Escape offers a restrained blood setting.

This is an early playtest: the new frontier systems, balance and animations need player feedback. The art is AI-generated illustration, and the new content does not implement the full long-term world and dynasty vision. Existing campaigns, Chapter One and the frontier use separate saves.
